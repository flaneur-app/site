# Privacy

Flâneur has no server. This page is the whole privacy policy of the app.

- **What Flâneur collects:** nothing. There are no analytics, no crash reports sent anywhere and no
  account with Flâneur.
- **Where requests go:** from your machine straight to the services you use, from your own IP
  address. No proxy sits in between.
- **Your sign-in:** when you connect an account, the session (cookies or tokens) is stored in your
  operating system's keyring, and only ever sent to the service it belongs to.
- **Local data:** your watch positions, the feeds you follow and your settings are kept in a local
  database in your user folder. Deleting the app's data deletes them.

What each service does with your activity is governed by that service's own privacy policy, as it
would be on its website.

This website itself is static and sets no cookies.
