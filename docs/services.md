# Services

Flâneur starts with three connectors. Each one lives in its own repository and declares what it
can do, so the app only offers what works.

| Service | Watch | Search | Subscriptions | History | Likes | Comments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | Planned | Planned | Planned | Planned | Planned | Planned |
| Odysee | Planned | Planned | Planned | Planned | Planned | Planned |
| RSS & Atom feeds | Planned | — | In Flâneur | In Flâneur | — | — |

"In Flâneur" means Flâneur keeps it on your machine, because feeds have no accounts.

## RSS & Atom

Any feed that links to videos can be followed. For sites that host their own videos behind a feed,
small _page resolvers_ find the video file on the item's page. Items that a site marks as
sensitive are labelled as such.

Service names on this page only describe compatibility. Flâneur is not affiliated with any of
these services.
